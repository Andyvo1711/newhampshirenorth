Several spas in the Concord area have begun publishing detailed, itemized service menus online, a response to years of customer complaints about add-on charges disclosed only after a treatment had already begun, when most customers feel they have little practical choice but to accept the additional cost.

Owners who have adopted the more transparent pricing model say the change has, if anything, improved customer trust and repeat business, even in cases where the upfront total is higher than customers might have assumed from base advertised prices. Several describe consulting with staff to standardize what previously varied by which technician a customer happened to see.

Consumer protection advocates say pricing transparency complaints remain among the most common issues raised about the wellness industry generally, and encourage customers to ask for a full written estimate before any service begins, particularly for treatments like facials or massages where "upgrade" options are frequently presented mid-appointment.
