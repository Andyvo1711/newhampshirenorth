What began as a small neighborhood gathering organized by a handful of local families has grown into one of the Mount Washington Valley's most anticipated winter traditions, drawing visitors from across the region for an evening walk lit entirely by hand-carried lanterns through the village center.

Organizers say the event has intentionally resisted growing into a large commercial festival, keeping the format simple and community-led rather than adding vendors or ticketed elements that might change its character. Local businesses along the route open their doors with warm lighting and simple refreshments, embracing the spirit of the walk without turning it into a shopping event.

Longtime residents describe the lantern walk as a rare moment when the valley's tourism economy and its year-round community come together comfortably, since the event is free, unticketed, and equally embraced by seasonal visitors and locals who have participated since its earliest, much smaller years.
