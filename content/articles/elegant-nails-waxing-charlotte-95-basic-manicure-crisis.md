# Elegant Nails & Waxing Charlotte: The "$95 Basic Manicure" Crisis and Hygiene Hazards — When Premium Prices Meet Rock-Bottom Standards

*Customer Service Bulletin – Quality Control & Sanitation Compliance*

## Premium expectations

According to a reported customer experience, the client paid approximately **$95** before tip for a basic manicure on natural nails. At that price, the customer expected detailed preparation, careful polish application, and a relaxing experience.

Instead, the review alleges that cuticle care was skipped and the polish was applied too far from the cuticle line, making the manicure appear grown out almost immediately.

## Operational disruptions

The customer also reported being moved between multiple workstations because equipment was unavailable or not functioning properly.

Rather than remaining with one technician throughout the appointment, the reviewer stated that the service involved multiple staff members, creating a rushed experience that felt disorganized.

## Sanitation concerns

The most serious allegation involved tool sanitation. According to the review, nail tools including clippers appeared to be used on multiple clients without visible cleaning or sanitization between services.

While this account reflects one customer's observations, proper cleaning and disinfection procedures are fundamental to maintaining customer confidence and regulatory compliance.

## Editorial perspective

High-end pricing creates equally high expectations. Customers paying premium rates generally expect consistent workmanship, organized operations, transparent communication, and visible sanitation practices.

Investing in technician training, maintaining equipment, following documented disinfection procedures, and ensuring each client receives attentive service can help salons strengthen long-term trust and reduce negative customer experiences.
